🚗 Car Front / Dashcam Setup

Windshield Suction Installation

  • Mounting: Heavy-duty suction bracket behind the rearview mirror, angled ~15° downward toward the asphalt.
  • Vibration Control: Vehicle chassis and suspension absorb micro-vibrations, providing stable video frames.
  • Power: 12V cigarette lighter adapter or USB-C dashcam hardwire kit.
  • Driver Hazard Alert: Audio tone transmitted via vehicle cabin stereo or dashboard unit buzzer.
  • Damage Prevention: Protects low-profile tires, alloy rims, steering alignment, and front suspension.
🏍️ Two-Wheeler / Bike Mount Setup

Handlebar & Helmet Installation

  • Mounting: Anti-vibration phone handlebar clamp (e.g. QuadLock / RAM mount) or helmet chin GoPro bracket.
  • Vibration Control: Rubber dampening isolators prevent image sensor wobble from motorcycle engine revs.
  • Power: 5V 2A handlebar USB charging socket or internal rechargeable lithium battery.
  • Rider Hazard Alert: Bluetooth intercom inside helmet (Sena / Cardo) or loud piezo buzzer.
  • Safety Criticality: High severity craters pose an immediate risk of handlebar jerk, front-wheel lockup, or fall.
